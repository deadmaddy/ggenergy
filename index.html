How much does solar installation in Alwar cost?

Your quote depends on system capacity, panel and inverter selection, mounting structure, wiring and roof conditions. Share a recent electricity bill and rooftop details for a site-specific estimate. Eligible PM Surya Ghar subsidy is considered separately from the installation price.

Are you a Tata Power Solaroof Channel Partner?

Yes. Global Green Energy, Alwar is a Channel Partner of Tata Power Solaroof. Contact us to discuss Tata solar panels, available system configurations and the applicable product warranties.

Can I get a PM Surya Ghar subsidy?

Eligible residential rooftop solar installations may qualify for central assistance of ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and up to ₹78,000 for 3 kW or higher. Eligibility, equipment requirements and approvals follow the current PM Surya Ghar portal rules. Subsidy is not automatic or guaranteed.

Do you help with JVVNL and net metering?

We guide customers through the relevant JVVNL application and documentation steps for grid-connected rooftop solar. Technical feasibility, metering, inspection and commissioning remain subject to the DISCOM's requirements and approval.

Will rooftop solar work during a power cut?

A standard grid-connected system normally shuts down during a grid outage for safety. If backup is a priority, ask us about a suitably designed hybrid or off-grid system with batteries.
